# AI Standards Hub

The AI Standards Hub is a UK-based knowledge and community platform focused on AI standardisation, operated by the Alan Turing Institute with UK Government funding and in partnership with the British Standards Institution (BSI) and the National Physical Laboratory (NPL). Its stated aim is to help organisations, policymakers, and standards practitioners engage with AI standards work.

## What the Hub offers

Based on the live homepage (verified via automated fetch on 2026-08-17), the Hub provides:

- A **searchable catalogue of 300+ AI-related standards**, filterable by criteria such as topic and development status.
- A companion catalogue of **relevant legislation, government strategies, and policy documents**.
- A way to find standards that are currently **open for public consultation** (i.e., in a comment period before finalisation).
- **Community features**: live and online events, discussion forums on standardisation topics, and other interactive tools available to registered members.
- **Free training materials**, including an introductory "Standards at a Glance" resource and a broader e-learning collection.
- A **blog and curated research feed** aggregating thought leadership and third-party research on AI standards and governance.
- Account registration, which unlocks the ability to follow specific standards for update notifications, comment in the forums, and access training content.
