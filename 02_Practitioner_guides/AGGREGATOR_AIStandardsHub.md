# AI Standards Hub

The AI Standards Hub is a UK-based knowledge and community platform focused on AI standardisation, launched by the Alan Turing Institute with UK Government funding and in partnership with the British Standards Institution (BSI) and the National Physical Laboratory (NPL). As of 2026-10-05 its homepage describes a change of leadership to NPL and BSI (with UKAS also named) and says the Hub will reopen soon; see "Latest developments" below. Its stated aim is to help organisations, policymakers, and standards practitioners engage with AI standards work.

## What the Hub offered (as captured 2026-08-17, before the transition notice)

Based on the homepage as fetched on 2026-08-17, the Hub then provided the items below. These features were not visible on the 2026-10-05 holding page and may be unavailable until the Hub reopens:

- A **searchable catalogue of 300+ AI-related standards** (count as stated on the 2026-08-17 homepage; unverified since), filterable by criteria such as topic and development status.
- A companion catalogue of **relevant legislation, government strategies, and policy documents**.
- A way to find standards that are currently **open for public consultation** (i.e., in a comment period before finalisation).
- **Community features**: live and online events, discussion forums on standardisation topics, and other interactive tools available to registered members.
- **Free training materials**, including an introductory "Standards at a Glance" resource and a broader e-learning collection.
- A **blog and curated research feed** aggregating thought leadership and third-party research on AI standards and governance.
- Account registration, which unlocks the ability to follow specific standards for update notifications, comment in the forums, and access training content.

## What this summary does not include

The material captured from this page at the time of retrieval consisted almost entirely of homepage navigation, sign-up prompts, cookie-consent dialogs, and feedback/report-abuse forms — it did not include the underlying content of the standards catalogue itself (i.e., which specific standards are listed, or their individual descriptions). This file therefore describes what the Hub is and what it offers as a service, rather than reproducing any list of standards, since that list was not present in the captured content. Readers who need the actual catalogue should visit the Hub's standards-search page directly.

No fabricated or unverifiable claims were identified in the retrievable content; the removals from this summary are limited to site navigation, cookie/GDPR consent UI, and user-feedback form boilerplate, none of which carries factual content worth preserving.

- **Hub is on a holding page.** On 2026-10-05 the homepage showed a notice that the Hub "will reopen soon" as leadership moves from the Alan Turing Institute to NPL and BSI, with UKAS also named as a leader and coordination with the AI Quality Infrastructure Consortium (AIQI). Source: https://aistandardshub.org/

The earlier "300+" figure is therefore unconfirmed. Source: attempted fetch of that URL.
